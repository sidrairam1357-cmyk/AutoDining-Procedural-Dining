Product overview

Your surface. A complete dining scene.

AutoDining works on the surface you already have. Pick a dining table, a counter or a buffet surface, press generate, and the add-on arranges plates, bowls, food, cutlery, glasses and table decorations across it.

  • Works on your existing surface. AutoDining does not create a table, and your original surface stays unchanged.
  • Complete setups, not single props. Food, tableware, cutlery, drinks and decor come together in one pass.
  • Made for artists. Replace individual objects, recolour materials and choose a quality level afterwards.

FAQ

Questions, answered

AutoDining is a Blender add-on for procedural food, tableware and event setup. It generates complete dining arrangements — food, plates, bowls, glasses, cutlery and decorations — on a surface you choose.

No. AutoDining does not create a table. You provide an existing surface — a dining table, counter or buffet surface — and the add-on generates the arrangement on top of it. Your original surface stays unchanged.

Select an existing surface in Blender, open the AutoDining panel, choose the arrangement and settings you want, and generate. You can then customize individual objects and the quality level.

Yes. After generating, you can replace individual items such as dishes, food, cutlery, drinks and decorations, and recolour materials such as the table runner and cloth. Arrangement randomization is also available.
